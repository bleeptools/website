4. Newsletter (sent via CleverReach)

You can sign up on this website for my newsletter to receive occasional news about bleeptools products (for example about betas, releases and updates).

Data processed

When you sign up, I process your email address. To prove your consent, I also store the date, time and IP address of your signup and of your confirmation.

Double opt-in

After signing up, you will receive an email with a confirmation link. I add you to the mailing list only after you confirm.

Legal basis

Emails are sent on the basis of your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. Logging the signup serves to prove your consent (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, Art. 7(1) GDPR).

Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future, for example via the unsubscribe link in every newsletter email or by emailing me. Processing carried out before your withdrawal remains lawful.

Retention

I store your data until you unsubscribe from the newsletter. After that, I delete your email address from the mailing list. To honour your unsubscription and to be able to document signup and unsubscription, I may keep the data in a suppression list or proof log for as long as claims may arise (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Service provider

I use CleverReach, a service of CleverReach GmbH & Co. KG, Schafjückenweg 2, 26180 Rastede, Germany, to send and manage the newsletter. Your data is stored on CleverReach's servers; according to CleverReach, the server locations are in Germany or Ireland (EU). I have concluded a data processing agreement with CleverReach under Art. 28 GDPR. More information: CleverReach's privacy information.

Performance measurement

The newsletter emails contain tracking pixels and tracking links used to evaluate whether and when you opened the email and which links you clicked. This is based on your separate consent, which you give when signing up (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG). You can withdraw it at any time.

6. YouTube videos

This website offers a video about the software, hosted on YouTube. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. The parent company is Google LLC, USA.

No connection to YouTube is made when you open a page. The player is only loaded after you open the video and click "Play video", and that choice applies only until you leave or reload the page. I use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com).

Once the player is loaded, YouTube processes data such as your IP address, the page you are on, information about your browser and device, and how you use the player, and it may store information on your device or read it from there. If you are signed in to a Google account, YouTube may be able to link this to your account. I have no influence over this processing.

Legal basis: your consent, given by clicking "Play video" (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG). You can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future by closing the video and reloading the page.

Transfers to third countries: Data may be transferred to the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

If you follow a video button without JavaScript enabled, it is a plain link to youtube.com, and YouTube's terms and privacy policy apply there.
